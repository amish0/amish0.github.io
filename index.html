About Me

Senior DevOps & MLOps Engineer | AI Deployment Specialist

I am a Senior Engineer (System Analyst) at Tata Consultancy Services, where I drive end-to-end DevOps and MLOps pipeline implementation for AI applications in manufacturing environments. My work focuses on Git-based workflows, CI/CD automation, release management, containerized deployment, Kubernetes orchestration, and production infrastructure support for reliable AI delivery.

With nearly five years of experience across DevOps, MLOps, AI deployment, and embedded systems, I have built scalable deployment platforms across development, validation, and production stages. I have implemented Jenkins and ArgoCD automation, Docker and Docker Swarm deployments, Kubernetes-based infrastructure, Linux server configuration, MLflow model lifecycle management, and robust release and rollback workflows aligned with SDLC standards.

My academic foundation includes a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from NIT Durgapur, with research in biomedical image analysis, deep learning, and medical image processing. This strong AI background complements my practical expertise in deploying computer vision and AI solutions in real-world industrial settings.

I enjoy turning research-driven prototypes into dependable, production-ready systems and building the operational backbone to make AI work reliably in the real world. My work combines engineering discipline, deployment automation, and cross-functional collaboration to improve delivery speed, system stability, and business impact.

Interesting Engineering Stories

A few engineering stories that reflect how I approach difficult problems: turning ideas into working systems, improving performance, and making AI and automation reliable in real-world environments.

Multi-camera

Multi-camera tracking at speed

multiple-webcam-multi-thread was built to push multiple video streams through parallel workers instead of a single blocking pipeline. The goal was simple: make real-time monitoring faster, smoother, and more scalable.

  • Parallel stream processing
  • Reduced latency in multi-video setups
  • Useful for surveillance and vision demos
Object tracking

Tracking is more than detection

The Tracker project focuses on persistent object identity across frames. In practical terms, it is about keeping track of the same object as it moves through crowded scenes and changing viewpoints.

  • Multiple tracker families
  • Identity association across frames
  • Useful for surveillance and intelligent monitoring
Robotics

Autonomous robot decision-making

swarm_boat is a strong example of embedded intelligence: a small autonomous boat combining sensing, motion control, obstacle avoidance, and telemetry to make decisions in real time.

  • ESP32 + motor control
  • Obstacle avoidance logic
  • Browser and telemetry-based monitoring

Smart traffic monitoring
